## To permanently delete your account:
Book Chapter Verse accounts use the Unity Player Account SDK to store your data.  To delete your account:
1. Visit the Unity Player Account login page.
	- https://player-login.unity.com
2. Sign in to your Unity Player Account.
3. Delete the account associated with Bible Chapter Verse.

Deleting your account permanently removes your cloud save data associated with Bible Chapter Verse.

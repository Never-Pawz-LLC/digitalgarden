## Welcome to BCV (Book Chapter Verse)
A unique and refreshing way to interact with the Bible. Receive a random Bible verse and guess its Book, Chapter and Verse. Challenge yourself, increasing your reward the closer you get with each consecutive guess. Use your rewards to encounter Bible relics and build your collections with a purchased Premium Account. Build a familiarity with the Bible with this challenging game.

	- People and creature collections.
	- Vertical Phone orientation.

	- Red Thread Collection
